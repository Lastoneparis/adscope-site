AdScope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

AdScope is a dashboard for your App Store keyword rankings, reviews and Apple Search Ads campaigns. It is built to keep your data on your device.

Data we collect

AdScope collects no personal data. There are no AdScope accounts, no AdScope servers, and no analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind. Your tracked apps, keywords, ranking history, competitors, review triage state and settings are stored only on your device (and your device's standard backups).

Credentials you provide

Every credential you enter is stored only in your device's Keychain, is used only to call the matching service directly from your device, and is never transmitted to us:

  • Apple Search Ads — client ID, team ID, key ID and private key, used to read and manage your campaigns.
  • App Store Connect API — issuer ID, key ID and private key, used to read your own crash and analytics reports.
  • Telegram — bot token and chat ID, if you turn on Telegram alerts (see below).

Deleting the app removes all of them.

Network requests

AdScope talks to Apple only, unless you deliberately connect Telegram. The Apple endpoints are:

  • the public iTunes Search API (keyword rankings, app and competitor metadata);
  • the public App Store feeds (chart positions, customer reviews);
  • the Apple Search Ads API and the Apple ID token endpoint (your campaigns, if connected);
  • the App Store Connect API (your own crash and analytics reports, if connected).

These requests are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The public requests carry no identifier of you — only the search terms, app identifiers and storefronts needed to answer them.

Telegram alerts (optional, off by default)

If you connect a Telegram bot, AdScope sends your alerts to Telegram's servers so they can reach your chat. Those messages contain the alert contents — app names, keywords, rank changes, storefronts, and the text of customer reviews if you enable review alerts. This is a third-party transfer that happens only after you enter a bot token, and it stops as soon as you disconnect. Telegram's handling of those messages is governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, AdScope can summarise your growth plan and suggest keywords using Apple's on-device foundation model. That runs entirely on your device: nothing is sent to us, to Apple or to any other server for these features.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled in your Apple ID settings at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes all local data, including Keychain credentials. Messages already delivered to your own Telegram chat are yours to delete in Telegram.

Children

AdScope is a professional tool for app developers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
